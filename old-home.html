ABOUT ME

Hi there, thanks for visiting. My name is Thiago Zeitune. I am a Brazilian, Israel based designer. My passion is bringing to people well thought-out products that make their life easier.

I have spent the last 2 years or so working at Phenom People, a B2B SaaS startup in talent acquisition, transitioning over different products such as a CRM platform, a bot and career websites, while collaborating with top teams across a variety of companies around the globe. Prior to that, I have worked as a Visual Designer at Aspire Global, an iGaming company and Studio Jeger, a branding and exhibition design studio.

My focus as a product designer is to bring user’s needs and behaviour to the center of the stage on every step of the product development, while just as importantly taking business and brand needs into consideration from the start. I love working with passionated cross-functional teams, learning new things and caring about details.

I earned a master's degree in Integrated Design from the Holon Institute of Technology (HIT) and previously graduated from the Federal University of Rio de Janeiro with a degree in Graphic Design.

I love playing the guitar with my 2 year-old son 🎸(Bossa Nova, Samba, Rock and Reggae are our favourites), playing volleyball and listening to podcasts about useful and useless stuff.
